# Shot list (Version C, 17 Nov 2026)

Every photo placeholder on the site, for the photographer. One half-day on a normal busy morning, documentary style. Warm natural light, one consistent colour grade, shot like a magazine feature, not an advertisement. Eye contact and real smiles. The Sherpa beside the member, never across the room. No mirrors, no posed flexing.

**Consent:** a written yes, with first name and age, from every member shown. No member photo goes live without it.

Orientation note: "landscape" photos run across the full width of the screen with the words below, so keep the subject in the middle band (the top and bottom are cropped differently on phones and desktop). "Portrait" photos are shown tall and narrow.
