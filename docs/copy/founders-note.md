## Approved text

We are four founders, aged 41 to 59. At this age, society expects you to start "slowing down", accepting the nagging back, the fading energy, or the shrinking horizon as a natural tax on aging. We chose a different path: the work of staying capable.

Whether you are navigating a wake-up call or protecting the high-functioning life you love, the goal is the same. You don’t wait for a fire to buy insurance; you build the foundation now so your future remains open. We build a Strength Reserve. That extra "gas in the tank" ensures you can lift a heavy suitcase, play three hours of pickleball, or climb Kilimanjaro at 80.

Stop surviving your schedule. Start practicing possibility.
