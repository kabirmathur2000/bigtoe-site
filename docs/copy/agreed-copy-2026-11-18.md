FAQ PAGE
1. Who is BigToe for?
Men and women in their 40s, 50s, 60s, 70s and beyond. Whether you're new to training, returning after a break or already active, we start with your ability and your goals. There is no one else's pace to keep up with.

2. I have an old injury or a health condition. Can I train?
Tell us in your intake form before your first visit, and check any exercise restrictions with your doctor. We already train members taking GLP-1 medication. We'll discuss what's appropriate for you before you begin.

3. Who trains me, and how do you decide what I need?
A dedicated coach, whom we call a Sherpa, guides you through every session. Your plan starts with the Reserve Audit: body composition, balance and mobility, functional strength, and conditioning. We review your progress together and adjust your training as you go.

4. How long is a session, and what does it cover?
Sessions are 60 minutes. Every one works on mobility, strength and conditioning together, making the most of your training time. We recommend training two to four times a week.

5. How many people train at the same time?
We train up to five members at a time, each one-to-one with their own Sherpa.

6. How do I schedule or change a session?
You'll have regular slots that are yours. Need to move one? Change it by the day before. And when you travel, train online or pause until you're back.

7. What does it cost?
₹25,000 to ₹50,000 a month, depending on how often you train. Your first hour is free. Nutrition support and blood work are available at an additional cost.

8. What if BigToe isn't for me?
Complete six sessions within three weeks of joining. Studio and online sessions both count. Not for you? Your money back, no questions asked.

9. Is there a community beyond training?
Yes. Quarterly Vinyl Nights, community runs, book readings, lifestyle talks and beer-and-pizza lunches. Different reasons to get together, share an interest and get to know the people you train alongside.

10. What happens at my first visit?
You'll meet a founder, have a body scan and a light assessment, and stay for coffee and a chat. It's a chance to talk about your goals and understand how we train. The team will confirm your preferred time.
